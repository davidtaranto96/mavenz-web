Where Salta is growing, and why

Six territories, and what is happening in each.

01Downtown and city centreWhere heritage begins to change hands

The centre stopped growing outwards and grew by transformation. Companies, studios and offices now sit next to cafés and shops, and the colonial houses have left the traditional residential market.

Profile and opportunities of the area Companies, professional practices, entrepreneurs, tourism and people who want urban, connected living.

02North sideThe city grows towards nature

One of the main expansion corridors in Salta. Every new development consolidates a residential ecosystem that keeps growing.

Profile and opportunities of the area Young families, professionals and anyone planning a long-term home without giving up the city.

03San LorenzoTwo territories, two ways of living

San Lorenzo Viejo is a consolidated market with a stable setting. San Lorenzo Chico is no longer only residential: schools, offices, gyms and restaurants let people live and work in the same place.

Profile and opportunities of the area Established families in Viejo. In Chico, young families, professionals and entrepreneurs who blend home, work and quality of life.

04Airport area and San LuisConnectivity that drives growth

The expansion southwards finds its engine here. Being close to the airport and the main access roads sustains steady demand, alongside new neighbourhoods, services and shops.

Profile and opportunities of the area Working families, professionals and people tied to commerce and logistics who value access.

05Cafayate and CachiInvesting is also choosing a way to live

The Calchaquí Valleys are a different market: the purchase decision mixes heritage, enjoyment and yield. Demand for second homes and tourism-linked investment keeps growing.

Profile and opportunities of the area People looking for a second home, heritage investors, tourism entrepreneurs and families who put experience and nature first.

06VaquerosAn eco-cultural answer

Vaqueros is emerging as an eco-cultural answer, more bohemian and open, with a strong sense of community. The new urban and environmental planning code and the widened access road reinforce the area.

Profile and opportunities of the area Couples aged 30 to 45 who work downtown or remotely and want their children to grow up close to nature. Artists, architects and designers. Short-stay rental investors.
